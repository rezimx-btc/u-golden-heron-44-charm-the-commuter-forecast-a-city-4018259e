One line of weather forecast for a city that does not exist: in Quibberton, the rain clocks in early — at 8:59, just like you — and the platform finally agrees.

![Quibberton at 8:59 — rain, a yellow umbrella, and a commuter who made it on time](image.png)

## The entry

> In Quibberton, rain is due at nine, but it clocks in at 8:59 — early, like you, and the platform finally agrees.

112 characters, one line, one invented city, one forecast, one small smile for the morning commute.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
